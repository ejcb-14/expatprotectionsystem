Coffee with Hospiten Paitilla · Panama City

Get to know Hospiten Paitilla before you ever need it

Join a small guided tour of Hospiten Paitilla for English-speaking expats, led by the hospital's International Department team. You will see how the hospital works for patients like you, and we finish with a short Q&A on health insurance in Panama.

During the visit you can join Hospiten Paitilla's Expat Global Care program for free.

No sales pitch. Just clear answers.

Free membership

Expat Global Care

During the visit you can join Hospiten Paitilla's Expat Global Care program for free, with a short form from Hospiten. The membership includes:

  • Health Navigator. Dedicated professionals to guide you at every step.
  • VIP treatment. Personalized attention and priority service.
  • Annual preventive health check-up. Included with the membership and scheduled with Hospiten after you enroll.
  • Medication delivery to your home. Within the Panama City metropolitan area.
  • Exclusive member health fairs. Health events designed for members.
  • Welcome kit. Given when you enroll.
